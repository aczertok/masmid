# Limud app: Orthodox content standards

The app must always follow Orthodox Judaism. Sefaria is a library run by a non-denominational organization. It hosts Orthodox and non-Orthodox works side by side, so every text is chosen deliberately from Orthodox editions only.

## 1. Approved editions only
Use only editions on this list. Anything not on it is not used, even if Sefaria offers it.

| Text | Hebrew | English |
|---|---|---|
| Mishnah | Vilna (Romm) 1913 | R' Shraga Silverstein, with Bartenura |
| Kitzur Shulchan Aruch | Torat Emet 357 (vowelized) | Metsudah, R' Avrohom Davis |
| Chumash with Rashi | Tanach with Nikkud | Metsudah Chumash and Metsudah Rashi |
| Nach (Yehoshua) | Tanach with Nikkud | Koren Jerusalem Bible (adapted at 2:1); Rashi: Metsudah |
| Gemara | William Davidson (Koren Steinsaltz) Aramaic | William Davidson (Koren Steinsaltz) English, a few lines adapted for modesty |

Never use:
- Sefaria Community Translations (anyone can edit them)
- Non-Orthodox translations (e.g. the Conservative Yeshiva's Mishnah Yomit by Dr. Joshua Kulp)
- Source sheets, modern essays, or machine translations

New subjects need an approved Orthodox edition added to this list before any content is built.

## 2. Rabbinic review
- Every lesson starts as "awaiting review."
- A named Rav reviews every card: the glosses, notes, questions, answers, and cartoon captions.
- Only approved lessons are shown to learners.
- Halacha content must be reviewed before any public release.

## 3. Explanations
- Notes should come from recognized mefarshim (Bartenura, Rashi, Mishnah Berurah, the Metsudah notes), credited by name.
- AI-written wording is limited to plain translation and question phrasing, and is reviewed like everything else.

## 4. Halacha
- Every lesson has "ask your Rav" notes. The app teaches; it does not pasken.
- Users choose their minhag (Ashkenaz, Sefard, Chassidish, Edot HaMizrach). Answers that follow a different accepted minhag are never marked wrong.

## 5. Presentation
- Hashem's Name: ה' and אלקים (not the full Name, not אלהים).
- Lesson cartoons follow tznius standards. They show only men, in modest dress. Hashem, angels, demons, Adam and Chava, and women are never drawn.
- Bodily or marital topics keep the exact Hebrew text, with minimal, modest English. They are never used in questions or pictures.
- Kitzur simanim 145, 148 and 150–162 are shown only to Man and Woman profiles, with no questions. Boy and Girl profiles see them as "learned with a parent or Rav."

## 6. Profiles
- Every learner picks a profile when starting: Man, Woman, Boy or Girl. It can be changed in Settings. It's on the honor system.
- The profile decides:
  - which avatar parts can be chosen (for example, sheitels and tichels for women; beards and peyos for men);
  - who appears walking around the maps, the town and the beis medrash rooms (people of the same profile);
  - who you can see and be seen by in "Learn together."
- Avatars are always modestly dressed. Married women's avatars always have covered hair.
- Most avatar parts and town buildings start locked and open through learning (lessons, XP, streaks, siyumim, badges).

## 7. Community ("Learn together" and Learners)
- Each profile has its own separate group. Men see only men, women only women, boys only boys, girls only girls. There is no mixed group.
- People are shown by a self-chosen nickname and their avatar only: no real names, photos, or free-text profiles.
- No chat or free text of any kind. The only interaction is a preset high five / "Yasher Koach", rate-limited.
- Only what someone is learning (and where they are on the map), their level, streak, badges and town are shared. Anyone can turn this off in Settings.
